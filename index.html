Starting

Find

  • Hold the screen and say what you want: "Find the door", "Where's a chair?", "Exit".
  • Turn slowly. Beeps get faster as you face it, and come from its side.
  • A chime and a voice tell you when you're facing it and how far it is.
  • Tap to hear where it is. Say "stop" to end.
