Starting

Find

  • Hold the screen and say what you want: "Find the door", "Where's a chair?", "Exit".
  • Turn in a circle. A voice says "turn left" or "turn right". Low beeps come from the side it's on; high beeps mean you're facing it.
  • Walk toward the high beeps. They speed up as you get closer.
  • Tap to hear where it is. Say "stop" to end.
